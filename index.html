Soalan Lazim (FAQ)

Kenapa bayaran setup sekali + langganan bulanan?

Setup merangkumi konfigurasi sistem, latihan staff (2 jam), dan template mesej klinik. Bulanan merangkumi kos hosting server laju, kemaskini keselamatan, backup data pesakit, dan sokongan teknikal terus. Tanpa bulanan, kos asal sistem cecah RM3,000+ sekali bayar.

Berapa nombor WhatsApp boleh disambung?

Pakej Basic menyokong 1 nombor WhatsApp utama (Kaunter). Pakej Full Suite menyokong sehingga 2–3 nombor (cth: Kaunter 1, Kaunter 2 & Doktor) dalam satu cawangan. Penyambungan pantas melalui imbasan QR.

WhatsApp guna API rasmi ke?

Kami menggunakan automasi WhatsApp Web (imbas QR seperti WhatsApp Web biasa). Kelebihannya tiada caj setiap mesej (Official API kenakan RM0.15–RM0.30 setiap perbualan). Sistem kami dilengkapi 'smart human delay' dan kawalan anti-spam untuk mengurangkan risiko sekatan.

Adakah Alisya memberi nasihat perubatan?

Tidak sama sekali. Alisya bertindak sebagai pembantu pentadbiran — menguruskan waktu temujanji, pendaftaran awal, dan peringatan susulan. Sebarang soalan klinikal atau ubat akan terus diserahkan kepada doktor bertugas.

Legal

Terms & Disclosure

Full terms for Klinik CRM packages — WhatsApp automation disclosure, PDPA consent, and medical disclaimer.

1. What technology we use

Klinik CRM uses WhatsApp Web automation — each number scans a QR code, and the system mirrors whatsapp.com sessions. This is not the Official WhatsApp Business API (Meta / green-tick API).

2. Why we chose it

Official API charges per conversation (est. RM0.15–0.30 per 24h window) plus BSP fees. Web automation has no per-message fee — that's why Starter is RM99/mo, not RM300+. Setup is 10 minutes via QR, no Meta business verification wait.

3. Risks you must know

WhatsApp's terms allow them to temp-block or ban numbers that behave like spam (bulk blasting, >30 unsolicited messages/hour, recipient reports). A ban affects your phone number, not our server — only WhatsApp Support can unblock. Our system does not bypass WhatsApp's encryption or limits.

4. How we mitigate

Warm-up: first 3 days ≤10 outbound messages/day per number, only to contacts who consented. Rate limit: ≤30 new conversations/hour per device, with a durable queue and retry. Backup: keep at least 2 numbers so one block never halts your clinic. Anti-spam: Alisya never sends duplicate messages (85% similarity guard).

5. Your responsibilities

Only message patients who gave consent (PDPA Malaysia). No purchased lists. Don't blast beyond the limits above. Keep the paired phone online for session sync.

6. Upgrade path

If you need the green-tick, template approvals, or >1,000 messages/day, we can migrate you to the Official WhatsApp Business API — additional Meta/BSP costs apply. Your patient data migrates, no rebuild.

7. Medical disclaimer

Alisya is an appointment assistant, not a doctor. No diagnosis, no dosage, no treatment promises. Clinical questions are always referred to your qualified doctor.

8. Acceptance

By signing the setup invoice, you confirm you have read this disclosure and accept the trade-off: lower cost now with manageable automation risk, versus higher cost with official guarantees.
